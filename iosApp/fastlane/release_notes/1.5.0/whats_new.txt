What's new in 1.5.0:

• Claim Guide (new Guide tab): short answers to common service-pay and claim questions for PCDA(O) officers, each with the rule it comes from. The title and the one-line answer are free; key points, the authority and the details are in Premium. You can also keep your own private notes on any card.
• TA/DA claim worksheet (Premium): work through a TA/DA claim step by step, with the documents to attach and the rates for your case, before you submit it.
• Leave accumulation: the December leave table in your payslip is now read and shown. The Retirement Calculators card uses it, and Smart Insights warns you when your leave is at the 300-day cap (Premium).
• Clearer layout at large font sizes, and a Guide that follows the app's light and dark theme.
• Backups now also carry your Guide notes.

Everything still runs 100% on your device. Your payslips are never uploaded.
