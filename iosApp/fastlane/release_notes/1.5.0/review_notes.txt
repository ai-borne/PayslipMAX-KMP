Dear App Review Team,

This is a feature update (v1.5.0) on top of the approved v1.3.0. There are no new permissions, no new data collection, no network calls added and no changes to the In-App Purchase.

WHAT CHANGED:

1. Claim Guide (new tab, bundled with the app, works offline): short rule cards on service-pay and claim questions for PCDA(O) officers. Each card cites the rule it is based on. Free users see the title and the one-line answer; key points, authority and details are part of the existing Premium subscription and lead to the existing paywall. Users can also write private notes on a card; notes stay on the device (encrypted) and are included in the existing password-protected backup file.

2. TA/DA claim worksheet (inside the Guide tab, Premium): a step-by-step worksheet that lists the documents to attach and the rates for the user's case. It is local calculation only.

3. Leave accumulation: the app now reads the December leave table printed on the user's imported payslip and shows it. The Retirement Calculators card and a Smart Insight (leave at the 300-day cap) use it. The card and insight are part of the existing Premium subscription.

4. Layout fixes for large font sizes and theme colours in the Guide.

IN-APP PURCHASE (unchanged since v1.2): the yearly auto-renewing subscription "payslipmax_yearly_premium" is offered from Settings > Upgrade to PayslipMax Premium. Restore Purchases is on the same sheet, and Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links are shown in its footer and in the App Description.

HOW TO TEST: the app requires no login or credentials. The Guide tab works without any payslip. To see the leave table, tap the Upload button on the Dashboard and choose a PCDA(O) payslip PDF for a December (it prints the leave accumulation table). The Guide is informational: it does not give financial or legal advice, and the app is not an official app of the PCDA, the Ministry of Defence or the Indian Army (stated in Settings > Help).

All parsing and calculations run locally on the device and no payslip data is transmitted anywhere.

Thank you for your time and review!
