Brutally Honest AI Insight Critique

Developer-only UX & retention evaluation. Critical review of what works, what is weak, and how to improve before release.

  • Valuable (The Arrears Audit): Extremely high signal. PCDA arrears calculations are notoriously opaque. Auditing them down to the rupee justifies the entire subscription cost instantly.
  • Valuable (Housing Rent Risk): Generates high emotional impact. Rent recoveries hit officers as sudden, heavy debits (often ₹50,000+). Catching this early is highly actionable.
  • Weak (Basic Pay Increment): It is technically correct, but low user value. The officer already knows they got an increment. It feels a bit like a generic summary. Better to frame it in terms of future retirement impact.
  • Weak (LTC Ticket Recovery): While catching the deduction is good, the prompt could connect it to specific travel booking dates or flag if the amount is mismatched with standard distance charts.
  • Suggested Additions:
    • Posting Change Alert: Automatically trigger when TPTA/DA/HRA changes suggest relocation (e.g. from Class A city to Field), prompting the user to submit posting forms.
    • DSOP Max Cap Warning: Alert if annual DSOP contribution exceeds the statutory limit (currently ₹5 Lakhs), preventing sudden tax penalization.
