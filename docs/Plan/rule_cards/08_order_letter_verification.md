## What this changes elsewhere

1. **Rate escalator.** The 15-09-2017 letter says its rates rise 25% each time DA crosses 50%. The FAQ's rupee figures include that
   rise. Cards must store base rate plus escalator and look up the effective date, not hard-code rupees.
2. **TR cards under amended rules.** The 2017-18 letters amend TR rules 57, 58, 60, 61, 62, 67, 69, 70, 92, 95, 107, 108, 114 and 245 (they
   name those rules in their headings). A card under any of these must draw its figures from the letter, not the TR 2014 text.
   TR cards currently affected: those under rules 67 (baggage and car) and 114 (DA); 61A (carriage of personal effects) likely falls under the letter's transfer/baggage section but is not named in its heading.
3. **Rule 67(d)(vii)** was rewritten by the 18-04-2018 letter (the letter says TR will be amended "in due course"). Any TR 67 card must carry that.
