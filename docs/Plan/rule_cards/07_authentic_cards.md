# Authentic rule cards: the definitive list

**Result: 62 cards ready to write** = 47 TR-anchored + 11 order cards verified against the primary letter + 4 order cards verified in substance (each with one claim to leave out). **5 cards are on hold** (primary letter not publicly found). 1 card removed. Plus **80 reserve rule-level cards** (TR + handbook only). Everything else in the 968-unit inventory is excluded until it clears the same bar.

## The bar (all must hold)
1. **The issuing office states its authority in the answer itself** (a TR rule, MoD/DoPT letter or Army Order). A rule number appearing only in the officer's question does not count.
2. **The authority exists in an independent document.** TR cards: the cited rule (or LTC sub-rule 177A-D) is a real rule in the TR 2014 *and* is independently cited by the Travelling Allowances Handbook. Order cards: the letter was located and its text read (see [08_order_letter_verification.md](08_order_letter_verification.md)).
3. **De-duplicated:** near-identical situations under the same rule are merged (1 merge).

Checked on top: for each TR card, the answer's vocabulary was compared with the cited TR rule's text (median 69% overlap); the lowest scorers were read by hand, and two that failed the bar (rule number only in the question) were dropped.

## What the numbers are, and are not
- **TR cards (47)**: primary text in hand (TR 2014). The TR is the 2014 edition and its rates are pre-7th-CPC. The 2017-18 MoD letters amend rules 57, 58, 60, 61, 62, 67, 69, 70, 92, 95, 107, 108, 114 and 245, so any card under those rules must take its figures from the letter, not the TR (see 08).
- **Order cards**: V = letter text read and the card's claim found in it (11); P = substance found, but one claim in the FAQ answer is not in the letter (4); U = letter not found publicly, on hold (5). Letters were read from published texts (a govt PDF for the DoE 2021 OM; PCDA(O)/CGDA forwarding letters and gazette republications for the rest), not downloaded from each ministry's own site.
- The 15-09-2017 MoD letter states base rates that rise 25% each time DA crosses 50%. The FAQ's rupee figures already include that escalation; cards must store the base rate plus the escalator.
- Not cross-referenced: the rest of the 116 travel and 135 pay FAQs state no authority in the answer; they are guidance, not authentic cards.

### Notes on P and U cards

- **AC-048 (U)**: MoD letter 67669/... dt 13-03-1973 not found publicly.
- **AC-049 (P)**: DoPT OM 29-08-2022 confirms one ticket per leg and ATA-only booking. It says nothing on en-route stay; omit that claim.
- **AC-052 (P)**: DoE OM 05-09-2014 confirms no fee outside the airline tariff. 'Excess baggage/food not admissible' is PCDA(O) inference, not in the OM; omit.
- **AC-055 (P)**: Letter base amounts: Levels 9-11 Rs 900, 12-13B Rs 1000, 14+ Rs 1200. FAQ's 1125/1250/1500 = base x1.25; verify the DA-50% escalation order and its effective date before showing rupee figures.
- **AC-059 (U)**: CGDA letter AT/IV/4462/OA/DSD/AF dt 13-09-2017 not found publicly.
- **AC-060 (U)**: AO 22/02 and SAO 4/S/88 are Army orders, not public.
- **AC-063 (P)**: DoE OM 15-06-2021 confirms 60 days (tour/transfer/training) and 180 days (retirement). The '30 days if advance drawn' clause is NOT in that OM; TR 17A(2) states an older regime. Card must omit the 30-day claim.
- **AC-064 (U)**: IHQ letter C/7036/HRA/SAPCS/2022 dt 06-09-2022 not found publicly.
- **AC-065 (U)**: MoD letters of 1991/1996 and DoPT OM 14034/01/2013-Estt(D) dt 03-10-2013 not located.
