The manual says "Required as prefix" for Data 1; a leave with no prefix is common, so the column is optional.

- Order number "----/----" (5.23, 5.24, 5.25 and the note on 5.3 col 9): read as the order number and its date, printed "no. dated date". The narrative and the Data column both print it this way.

- 5.24 VOLREJ and 5.25 RECALL: the manual writes "Annual/Furlough Leave" in the narrative. The template asks the officer for the kind of leave and prints it, as HRMS does ("Rejoined from CL/AL").
- Ration Money Allowance certificate (note 8, p.99) on GTDRSHNA: items 1 to 5 are printed. Item 6 (posted to a non-military station) and item 7 (prior approval of the Station Commander) are conditional and are left out; the clerk adds them where they apply. Item 1 takes the kind of leave as typed.
- Ration four dates: SORS and GTDRSHNA from the first day away (the earlier of the first prefix day and the first day of leave); TORS and STPRSHNA from the day after the last day away (the later of the last day of leave and the last suffix day). An extension (EXTNAL), voluntary rejoining or recall does not move them; the clerk reissues them.
- R&H (practice only, owner decision 2026-10-23): the stop and the re-grant link from annual leave and annual leave carried forward only, never from casual leave. The re-grant is under the officer's own GTDRH code and prints the station, rate, Corps notification and serial from p.46-48. Data 3 prints "Corps, notification no., date" in that order; the manual does not give the exact HRMS format.
- Checks (owner decision 2026-10-23): acknowledged before printing (ack): annual leave over 60 days a year (note 2) and casual leave beside another leave (p.117). Shown only (warn): prefix and suffix over 5 days (note 9) and a grant order not yet quoted (notes 7 and 2).
- Casual leave beside another leave is checked against the annual set only (GTDAL, GTDALCF, EXTNAL); the rest of Chapter 5 joins the check in phase 8.
