# SSOT rule list - travel (TD, permanent move, LTC, claims)

One list: **67 topics, 347 entries**. Entries are the distinct situations and rules found across the PCDA(O) FAQ, the TA Handbook and the TR 2014; true duplicates are merged (13 handbook paragraphs folded into the FAQ they restate). Entry text (the simplified answer, in our own words) is the next phase; this list fixes *what* goes in the app and *what supports it*. Machine-readable: [ssot.json](ssot.json).

Support mix (travel + pay): strong 109, good 169, basic 196.

`TR` = TR 2014 rule(s) the entry cites or its topic rests on. `LET` = MoD/MoF/DoPT letter named in the source; `[V/P/U]` = primary-letter check from [08](08_order_letter_verification.md) (V verified, P partly, U letter not found). `FAQ` = PCDA(O) website FAQ. `TA` = Travelling Allowances Handbook paragraph. `PA` = Pay & Allowances Handbook topic.
`Support` strong = an explicit TR rule or letter plus at least two more layers; good = explicit authority or three layers; basic = fewer. It is a label for how much backing an entry has, **not** a filter: every entry is in the list.
`RATES` = contains rupee/weight figures; take the base rate plus the DA escalator from the letter. `AMENDED` = under a TR rule that the 2017-18 MoD letters amend; figures come from the letter, not the TR 2014 text.
