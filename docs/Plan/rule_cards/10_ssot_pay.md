# SSOT rule list - pay and allowances

**95 rule-bearing topics** from the Pay & Allowances Handbook index, of which **19 have 127 FAQ entries** attached. The other 76 topics are handbook-only: their rules sit in tables and prose that still need to be broken into entries (next phase), so they appear as placeholders. Tax, insurance, pension and ECHS chapters are deferred (see [06](06_register_pay.md)). Machine-readable: [ssot.json](ssot.json).

`TR` = TR 2014 rule(s) the entry cites or its topic rests on. `LET` = MoD/MoF/DoPT letter named in the source; `[V/P/U]` = primary-letter check from [08](08_order_letter_verification.md) (V verified, P partly, U letter not found). `FAQ` = PCDA(O) website FAQ. `TA` = Travelling Allowances Handbook paragraph. `PA` = Pay & Allowances Handbook topic.
`Support` strong = an explicit TR rule or letter plus at least two more layers; good = explicit authority or three layers; basic = fewer. It is a label for how much backing an entry has, **not** a filter: every entry is in the list.
