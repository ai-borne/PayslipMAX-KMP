- Applies to NCC lady officers and MNS

Cite: MoD letter B/33922/AG/PS-2(b)/943/D(AG) dt 16-03-2009; Rule 56, Leave Rules for the Services Vol I (Army)

##### Child Care Leave for Female and Single Male Officers (2)

#### RB-RP-020 Child care leave: how much and for which children?

**Permanent commission women get 360 days, SSC women 180 days, over the whole career for two eldest children under 18.**

- Single male officers and fathers now eligible too
- Adoptee mothers also eligible

Cite: Rule 56A, Leave Rules for the Services Vol I (Army); MoD letters B/33922/AG/PS-2(b)/687/D(AG) dt 04-03-2014 and /1447/D(AG) dt 14-08-2019

<sub>Details: Single male means unmarried, widower or divorcee. NCC whole-time lady officers: two eldest minor children, up to 730 days in service. SSC officers later given PC receive the difference up to 360 days.</sub>

#### RB-C8-14 Child care leave: spells, limits and combinations

**At most two spells a year, 5 to 30 days each, up to 60 days a year. It is not debited to other leave.**

- Combine with any leave except casual leave
- Not granted during probation or training
- LTC allowed during CCL for women officers

Cite: Rule 56A, Leave Rules for the Services Vol I (Army); MoD letter B/33922/AG/PS-2(b)/3080/D(AG-II) dt 19-11-2018

##### Paternity Leave on birth of a child (1)

#### RB-RP-022 Paternity leave on birth or adoption

**Male officers with fewer than two living children get 15 days, on a birth or on adopting a child under one year.**

**Up to 24 months for approved non-academic courses, extendable to 28 with annual and furlough leave, at most twice in service.**

##### Encashment of leave (3)

#### RB-RP-028 How much leave can I encash?

**Up to 300 days over service, with at most 30 days accumulated a year, based on annual leave at credit.**

- Year of retirement: 15 days if retiring 31 Jan, else 30
- SSC: release year's leave is not encashable
- Cashiered or dismissed: no encashment

#### RB-C8-21 Leave encashment on invalidment or death in service

**Invalided: unused annual leave of that year up to 30 days. Death in January or February: about half the leave due.**

- No cut for pension equivalent of DCRG

Cite: MoD letter B/33931/AG/PS-2(b)/1908/D(AG) dt 12-04-2010

<sub>Details: Fractions are rounded to the nearest day. February deaths are worked out the same way.</sub>

**A joiner on 30 or 31 December gets no accumulation that year. Re-employment leave cannot be accumulated.**

- Commissioned before 1 Dec, joined early Dec: up to 15 days
- Civil deputation: PCDA(O) pays from Defence Estimates

Cite: AG's Br AHQ letter B/32238/AG/PS-2(b) dt 03-04-1998; MoD letter B/33931/AG/PS-2(b)/1364/2015/D(AG) dt 06-11-2015

##### Encashment of leave on LTC (4)

**Up to 10 days of annual leave per LTC, 60 days in all over your career, including any re-employment.**

##### Joining Time (3)

#### RB-RP-030 How much joining time do I get on a permanent posting?

**Six days preparatory time plus journey time: one day per 500 km by rail, 250 km by road, 350 km by sea.**

- Air: actual time, part day counts as one
- Distance is actual, not the railway's assumed distance
- Counted as duty; Sundays ignored but count toward 30-day limit

Cite: Para 18, AO 259/73

<sub>Details: Journey by river is 150 km a day and by other means 25 km. The competent authority may extend joining time up to 30 days. Casual leave may be granted in place of joining time but the two cannot be combined. Joining time is intended for winding up and re-establishing the household.</sub>

#### RB-C9-02 Joining time for a course or temporary duty

**Admissible for courses over 6 months only if you give up service accommodation. Otherwise only the journey time.**

- No 6 days preparation from a field area
- Not on return to the same station
- Newly commissioned: actual journey only on first posting

Cite: Paras 5, 10 and 19, AO 259/73; CGDA letter 1223/AT-P dt 06-07-1973

<sub>Details: If posted on permanent duty while on TD or a course, joining time counts from that station. Combined with annual leave and furlough: annual leave first, furlough next, then joining time.</sub>

#### RB-C9-03 Joining time reckoning, sickness, civil posting and handing-over days

**Handing or taking over: 7 days with a stores check (10 if dispersed), 4 days otherwise, Sundays and holidays included.**

- Not when joining time is joined with annual leave on transfer
- Not for interchange of posts within one unit
- Transfer to civil or foreign service: the borrowing body's rules

Cite: AO 310/73; Paras 7, 11 and 19, AO 259/73

<sub>Details: Falling sick in joining time and recovering within sick-list concession lets you take the balance. With annual leave on transfer, joining time runs from the old station, or the leave station if that gives less.</sub>

- First increment in new rank comes after 6 months
- Decide within 3 months; it is final

<sub>Details: Those promoted 01-01-2016 to 26-02-2019 had 6 months from 26-02-2019 to opt; promotions after that have 3 months. Example: Captain at Rs 69,000 promoted 10-12-2022 to Major, DNI 01-01-2023: Rs 69,400 until DNI, Rs 73,600 on DNI, Rs 75,800 on 01-07-2023.</sub>

**Pay is fixed notionally from the antedate, with increments added; the result is your pay on commission.**

- Increments in the antedate period are notional
- Next increment may fall soon after commission

Cite: Army Officers Pay Rules 2017, SRO 12(E) dt 03-05-2017
