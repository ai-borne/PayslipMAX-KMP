T: Dearness Allowance: when is it revised and what counts as pay?
A: DA is revised twice a year, from 1 January and 1 July, on matrix pay plus MSP and NPA only.
K: No other special pay is included
K: Paid on leave except leave without pay and allowances
K: Leave DA is based on leave salary actually drawn
C: Army Officers Pay Rules 2017, SRO 12(E) dt 03-05-2017
D: Rates from each date: 01-07-2016 2%; 01-01-2017 4%; 01-07-2017 5%; 01-01-2018 7%; 01-07-2018 9%; 01-01-2019 12%; 01-07-2019 17%; 01-01-2020 21%; 01-07-2020 24%; 01-01-2021 28%; 01-07-2021 31%; 01-01-2022 34%; 01-07-2022 38%; 01-01-2023 42%; 01-07-2023 46%; 01-01-2024 50%; 01-07-2024 53%; 01-01-2025 55%; 01-07-2025 58%; 01-01-2026 60%. Rates of 2020 to mid-2021 were frozen.
O: 53%, 55% and 58% confirmed from MoD DMA letters (1(6)/2021-D(Pay/Services)-Pt-I; 58% letter dt 09-10-2025, MoF OM 06-10-2025). 60% from 01-01-2026 is from MoF DoE OM dt 22-04-2026 (read via a government circular); the MoD letter for 60% was not seen. Next revision (01-07-2026) not yet issued.

=== topic=RP-057 from=RP-057
T: Special Compensatory (Counter Insurgency) Allowance: who and how much?
A: Troops on counter insurgency operations: Rs 16,900 in field areas, Rs 13,013 in modified field areas, Rs 10,500 in peace areas.
K: Base rates from 01-07-2017; rise 25% per 50% DA
K: Local unit needs commander's certificate of actual CI duty
K: Under 30 days: paid pro rata
C: MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017 and 14-09-2018; MoD letter 37269/AG/PS3(a)/90/D(Pay/Services) dt 13-01-1994
D: Operations named in the handbook: Rakshak, Rhino, Orchid and Hifazat I. No other area-based allowance is paid, but High Altitude Allowance is. Army officers in BRO units in J&K and the North East are not involved in CI operations and do not get it (MoD DO letter 16(1)/2006/D(Pay/Services) dt 02-07-2008).

=== topic=RP-059 from=RP-059
T: High Altitude Allowance: who qualifies and how much?
A: Posted to units at 9,000 feet or more. Monthly: Category I Rs 3,400, Category II Rs 5,300, Category III Rs 25,000.
K: Cat I: 9,000 to 15,000 feet; Cat II: above 15,000 feet
K: Cat III: 14,000 to 19,000 feet in notified difficult areas
K: Base rates from 01-07-2017; rise 25% per 50% DA
C: MoD letter F 69/3/75/D(Pay/Services) dt 28-02-1976; Rule 173, Pay and Allowances Regulations for the Army; MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017
D: Paid on permanent posting, on detachment or attachment over 14 days without DA, and in transit between qualifying areas. Not paid on annual or sick leave, or on duty elsewhere for over 14 days. Absence up to 14 days on sick list, casual leave or TD keeps it. Also paid with SCCIA and field area allowances, not with Siachen Allowance. MNS officers get the same.

=== topic=RP-062 from=RP-062
T: Deputation (Duty) Allowance: who gets it and how much?
A: For temporary transfer outside the regular line. Choose 50% of civil rates with service concessions or 100% without them.
K: Same station with concessions: 2.5%, max Rs 2,250
K: Change of station with concessions: 5%, max Rs 4,500
K: Without concessions: 5% or 10%, max Rs 4,500 or 9,000
C: MoD letter 13(1)/87/D(Pay/Services) dt 25-09-1987; MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017 and 14-09-2018
D: Percent is of basic pay; ceilings rise 25% per 50% DA, from 01-07-2017. Special pays like parachute or flying are not allowed with it unless the sanction says so; Technical, Specialist, NPA and Qualification Pay continue. Also payable to officers in civil employ, Estt 22 (SFF), Coast Guard and Assam Rifles. Not with Training Allowance or Special Security Allowance.

A: When a DA rise is announced late, you are owed the difference back to its start date.
K: DA rises from 1 January and 1 July
K: Check each month from that date to the first payment
K: The rise applies to matrix pay plus MSP and NPA only
C: Army Officers Pay Rules 2017, SRO 12(E) dt 03-05-2017
