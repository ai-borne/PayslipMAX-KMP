=== topic=RP-038 from=SS-P030,SS-P031
T: Technical Allowance: who gets Tier I and Tier II?
A: Technically qualified officers on full-time technical duties. Tier I after the young officers' technical course; Tier II after a prescribed course (one course per tier).
K: Tier I base Rs 3,000 a month from 01-04-2018
K: Tier II base Rs 4,500 a month from 01-04-2018
K: Tier II needs substantive Captain rank and a Tier II course
C: MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017 and 14-09-2018; MoD letter 15(49)/2017/Tech Allce/D(GS-II) dt 25-06-2018
D: Both limbs apply. Tier II needs substantive Captain rank and a prescribed Tier II course, with Service HQ detailment. Not for AMC, ADC, AEC, MNS or non-technical officers.
O: Handbook text prints no DA escalation clause for Technical Allowance; the earlier "rises 25%" bullet was removed. Check the 18-09-2017 letter.

=== topic=RP-038 from=SS-P029
T: Who is not entitled to Technical Allowance?
A: AMC, ADC, AEC and MNS officers, and non-technical officers, get neither tier.
K: Also not paid if posted against a non-technical post
K: Courses with no direct link to duties do not qualify
C: MoD letter 15(49)/2017/Tech Allce/D(GS-II) dt 25-06-2018

=== topic=RP-038 from=SS-P026
T: Technical Allowance for re-employed officers
A: Only while you are working full time on technical and upkeep duties and meet every eligibility condition.
K: Re-employment alone does not give the allowance
C: MoD letter 1(26)/97/XXII/D(Pay/Services) dt 29-02-2000

=== topic=RP-039 from=SS-P035,SS-P032,SS-P033
T: Specialist Allowance: who is eligible?
A: AMC and ADC officers graded by DGAFMS as Graded or Classified Specialist or Consultant, who are in the pool or hold a specialist post.
K: Graded Rs 3,600, Classified Rs 4,500, Consultant Rs 5,400 monthly
K: Base rates from 01-07-2017; rise 25% per 50% DA
K: Not for Brigadier and above (AMC) or Major General and above (ADC)
C: Rules 88 to 91, Pay and Allowances Regulations for the Army; MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017

=== topic=RP-039 from=SS-P034
T: When is Specialist Allowance not payable?
A: In transit between specialist posts, under arrest or suspension, on study leave or a course of 10 weeks or more.
K: Restored after suspension only if honourably acquitted
K: Still payable in these cases if you stay in the specialist pool
C: Rules 88 to 91, Pay and Allowances Regulations for the Army

=== topic=RP-040 from=SS-P036
T: Post Graduate Allowance: who gets it?
A: AMC and ADC officers with a postgraduate degree or diploma who are not eligible for Specialist Allowance.
K: Degree Rs 2,250 and diploma Rs 1,350 a month
K: Base rates from 01-07-2017; rise 25% per 50% DA
K: Must be on the DGAFMS pool list
H: Part II order citing the DGAFMS letter
C: MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017 and 14-09-2018

=== topic=RP-041 from=SS-P041
T: Who gets Flying Allowance?
A: Army Aviation officers in sanctioned posts of the permanent aviation cadre, including those on staff or ERE duty.
K: Base Rs 25,000 a month for all ranks from 01-07-2017
K: Rises 25% each time DA rises by 50%
K: Must meet the eligibility conditions
C: Rule 164, Pay and Allowances Regulations for the Army; MoD letter 1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-09-2017 and 14-09-2018

=== topic=RP-041 from=SS-P037
T: Army aviator at Siachen: Siachen or Flying Allowance?
A: One or the other, at your choice. Both are not paid together.
K: Rule applies from 01-04-1999
C: MoD letter 3(4)/97/D(Pay/Services) dt 28-05-1999

=== topic=RP-041 from=SS-P038

A: Payable for a non-flying course if your vacancy stays unfilled and you return to AOP duty.
K: Both conditions must hold
C: MoD letter B/26243/AG/PS3(a)/647/D(Pay/Services) dt 18-03-1993
