=== topic=RP-005 from=SS-P012,SS-P007,SS-P013
T: Promotion pay fixation: which option and how to opt
A: By default pay is fixed on the promotion date. You may opt for fixation on the next increment date.
K: Opt within 3 months of promotion or IHQ order
K: Part II order with occurrence code OPTFXDNI is needed
H: Option form signed by you and countersigned
W: Not available for deputation, direct recruitment or ad-hoc promotion
C: Army Pay Rules 2017 (7th CPC), option for pay fixation on promotion; MoD OM 1(20)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 26-02-2019

=== topic=RP-005 from=SS-P008,SS-P009
T: Can I change or late-file my promotion pay fixation option?
A: No. The option is final once made, and a late option is not accepted.
K: No option in time: pay is fixed from the date of promotion
K: No later amendment of an option already exercised
C: Army Pay Rules 2017 (7th CPC), option for pay fixation on promotion
O: FAQ-only on finality; primary rule not located.

=== topic=RP-005 from=SS-P010
T: Why was pay recovered after I opted for DNI fixation?
A: The default fixation from the date of promotion is cancelled up to the next increment date.
K: Promotion date to DNI: next higher cell in the new level
K: On DNI: two notional increments in the old level, then fix
K: Earlier default fixation is recovered up to DNI
C: Army Pay Rules 2017 (7th CPC), option for pay fixation on promotion

=== topic=RP-005 from=SS-P014
T: When is my first increment after promotion fixation?
A: After 6 months, on the next 1 January or 1 July. Later increments follow every 12 months.
K: Applies to promotions on or after 01-01-2016
K: Only one increment a year: 1 January or 1 July
C: Army Pay Rules 2017 (7th CPC); SRO 12(E) dt 03-05-2017

=== topic=RP-005 from=SS-P011
T: What document goes with the OPTFXDNI Part II order?
A: The option form signed by you and countersigned by the competent authority.
K: Both documents are forwarded together to PCDA(O)
C: Army Pay Rules 2017 (7th CPC), option for pay fixation on promotion
O: FAQ-only; primary rule not located.

=== topic=RP-006 from=SS-P018,SS-P016
T: Special increments for sports: who qualifies and how many?
A: Excellence at national or international events: 1 increment for national, 2 for international, maximum 5 in a career.
K: National: first to third in athletics, first only in games
K: Starts from the 1st of the month after the event
K: Government sanction needed in each case
C: MoD letter B/25571/AG/PS-3(b)/3335/D(Pay/Services) dt 04-10-1991; MoD letter 30(13)/2007/D(Pay/Services) dt 30-08-2017 and 24-08-2018, corrigendum dt 17-09-2021
O: FAQ also cites DoPT OM 03-10-2013 and MoD letter 1272 dt 19-08-1996; not read in full.
D: Referees and umpires accepted by an international federation can get two increments per event, within the same five-increment cap.

=== topic=RP-006 from=SS-P015
T: Do sports increments count for pay fixation or allowances?
A: No. They do not count for pay fixation, retirement benefits or allowances such as HRA.
K: Paid as personal pay by level
K: Not counted for promotion fixation
C: DoPT OM 6/1/2017-Estt(Pay-I) dt 11-06-2018, para 5
D: Personal pay per month: Level 10: 1,240; 11: 1,470; 12: 1,690; 13: 1,800; 13A: 2,030; 14 and 15: 2,250.

=== topic=RP-006 from=SS-P017
T: Time limit to claim a sports special increment
A: Claim within 6 months of the end of the sporting event.
K: Counted from completion of the overall event
C: DoPT OM 6/1/2017-Estt(Pay-I) dt 11-06-2018, para 6
