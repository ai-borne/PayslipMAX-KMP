# Orders batch B: supporting documents, time limits, procedure, island postings, families, Form 'D' (SS-T197..SS-T252).

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T197 chips=GUIDANCE
T: I lost my LTC boarding passes
A: Boarding passes are mandatory for LTC claims. Get a certificate from the airline with the travel details.
K: It must give passenger name, date, class, seat number and amount paid
K: Submit it to PCDA(O) with the claim

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T198 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Taxi on LTC to a place with no public transport
A: Reimbursed up to 100 km, capped at the warrant cost, if no public or private transport exists.
K: Only when the taxi continues an air or train journey
K: Give a self-certificate with the taxi number
W: Not reimbursed where private transport is available

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T199 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Ration money deducted from my TD claim
A: It is not deducted if you attach a non-drawal certificate from the QM of your serving unit.
K: The certificate must come from your own unit
W: A certificate from the TD station is not valid

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T201 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Signing certificates on a claim: your responsibility
A: You are personally responsible for every fact in a certificate you sign, and liable for false statements.
K: Certify only what you know and believe
K: If the form does not fit, amend it or do not sign

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T203,SS-T204,SS-T205 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Declarations on a TD claim
A: Declare you used no warrant for the claimed portion, were present on DA days, and had no free board.
K: No railway warrant or concession voucher used for that portion
K: Actually present at the outstation on DA days; no leave taken
K: No free boarding or lodging provided

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T206 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Form 'D' for army officers in civil employ
A: Army officers in civil employ on service pay can use Form 'D'. The cost is debited to the civil department.
K: Issued by the nearest SSO or OC Station
K: Needs the Head of Department's certificate of service pay

=== topic=RR-ORD-10 from=SS-T207,SS-T224
T: LTC to Lakshadweep: how to travel
A: Use SPORTS Samudram packages on Lakshadweep Administration ships. Only transport cost is reimbursed.
K: Claim the SPORTS website rate, or less if claimed less
K: Only the transportation cost, not accommodation
K: Fare follows your sea or river steamer entitlement
C: MoD letter 12647/LTC/SPORTS/Mov C/22/D(Mov)/2019 dt 22-03-2019; DoPT OM 31011/10/2017-Estt.A-IV dt 11-10-2018; CGDA letter AT/IV/4462/OA/DSD/AF dt 13-09-2017
D: SPORTS must certify the fare components and that you and your family actually travelled. Applies from 11-10-2018.

=== topic=RR-ORD-07 from=SS-T208
T: Leave encashment with LTC: how is it paid?
A: Ledger Wing pays it on your unit's Part II order, with your claim in the prescribed proforma.
K: The Part II order shows the warrant used or LTC availed
K: T Wing sections only deal with the LTC claim and title
C: Documentation Procedure for Publication of Part II Orders (Officers) 2014

=== topic=RR-ORD-08 from=SS-T211 chips=GUIDANCE
T: A TA claim pending for over a year
A: The Head of Department investigates. If genuine with valid reasons for delay, it is paid after usual checks.
K: Needs supporting documents
K: Paid by the Drawing and Disbursing or Accounts Officer

=== topic=RR-ORD-08 from=SS-T214
T: Family travelled separately: how long do I have to claim?
A: Each journey has its own 60 days. Baggage and car claims run from delivery at the new station.
K: Dates are reckoned separately for each journey
K: Baggage and car: 60 days from delivery to you
C: GFR 2017 Rule 290; MoF DoE OM 19030/1/2017-E.IV dt 13-03-2018

=== topic=RR-ORD-08 from=SS-T215,SS-T216,SS-T217
T: Which date counts as the date of submission?
A: Your own Controlling Officer: the date the claim reaches PCDA(O). Otherwise: the date it reaches your Controlling Officer.
K: A late treasury claim counts from your head of office's date
W: These dating rules do not apply to LTC claims
C: MoF DoE OM 19030/1/2017-E.IV dt 13-03-2018

=== topic=RR-ORD-16 from=SS-T213
T: Field area posting: where does my family live?
A: Choose within two months of posting: keep old accommodation, move the family to SPR or home town, or to separated family quarters.
K: Two months, extendable to three in individual cases
K: Applies to officers posted to field areas
C: SAO 10/S/86

=== topic=RR-ORD-09 from=SS-T218 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Writing to PCDA(O): how?
A: Send queries through your CO or OC, one officer and one subject per letter, online on the PCDA(O) website.
K: Give rank, name, personal number and CDA(O) account number
K: No OP IMMEDIATE or PRIORITY labels
K: Only actual cases, not hypothetical ones

=== topic=RR-ORD-09 from=SS-T219,SS-T222 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Booking through DTS: advance and claim rules
A: DTS tickets count as an advance. The 60-day limit and penal interest apply.
K: Show ticket amounts on both credit and debit sides
K: Attach ticket copies and boarding passes
K: DTS also covers the DA and accommodation advance

=== topic=RR-ORD-09 from=SS-T220 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Applying for an advance online
A: Requisition TD, move or LTC advances online on the PCDA(O) website, separately for each.
K: Log in with your user ID and password
K: DTS claims go preferably through the DTS portal

=== topic=RR-ORD-09 from=SS-T221 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Refunding government dues to PCDA(O)
A: Remit by e-MRO or MRO payable to PCDA(O) Pune alone, quoting its RBI code and the reason.
K: e-MRO through the SBI portal is preferred
K: State on what account the amount is remitted
D: PCDA(O) Pune RBI code: 4021000009.

=== topic=RR-TD-03 from=SS-T222 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Courses held and attended locally: TA and DA?
A: Not payable for courses outside authorised Category A and B establishments. Daily Messing Allowance applies.
K: Officers on such courses get free rations

=== topic=RR-ORD-09 from=SS-T223
T: Official travel abroad: tickets and claims
A: PCDA(O) does not pay air fare where an air warrant is mandatory, but does pay DA and incidentals.
K: PCDA(O) pays the advance and final claims for TA and DA abroad
K: PCDA New Delhi deals with air passage claims for ex-India travel
C: Para 242 of the Defence Audit Code

=== topic=RR-ORD-12 from=SS-T232 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Married after taking LTC this year: spouse's LTC?
A: Your spouse gets LTC both ways to the same leave station, within six months, even without you.
K: You must already have taken your own LTC for the year

=== topic=RR-ORD-12 from=SS-T233
T: Officer under suspension: family's LTC?
A: You get no LTC, but your family can take LTC independently, if otherwise due.
K: A suspended officer cannot be granted leave
C: CGDA letter 4308/AT/P dt 28-03-1970

=== topic=RR-ORD-12 from=SS-T234
T: Family's Home Town LTC: another station?
A: Only the home town, even if another station is nearer or cheaper.
K: Applies to the wife and other family members
C: CGDA letter 4007/AT-P dt 05-05-1976

=== topic=RR-ORD-12 from=SS-T235
T: I travel on Form 'D' without LTC: my spouse's title?
A: Your spouse's independent title stands, if you claim no LTC for that journey.
K: Applies when you travel at your own expense using Form 'D'
C: AO 531/1964

=== topic=RR-ORD-13 from=SS-T238,SS-T140,SS-T240
T: Changing my declared home town
A: Once declared, the home town is final. A change is allowed once in service, in exceptional cases.
K: Part II order quoting the AG's Branch letter; effective from its date
K: Head of Department's sanction, or MoD's, goes with the LTC claim
W: Administrative authorities check warrants against your recorded home station
C: AO 22/02; SAO 4/S/88; CGDA clarification under Rule 190(5)(ii), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-ORD-15 from=SS-T242,SS-T243,SS-T244,SS-T245,SS-T246
T: Form 'D': families, chair car, joining time
A: Unaccompanied families may use Form 'D' to any station. You may use it anywhere in the country on leave.
K: One extra set lets them break the journey, at your cost
K: AC chair car: Form 'D' at 60% of the actual fare
W: Not during joining time, which is not leave
C: MoD letters PT/1203/NHQ/4851/D(Mov) dt 12-09-1974 and G/05492/Q Mov C/3853 D(Mov) dt 25-09-1979; CGDA letters 4007/AT-P and AT/IV/4425/1

=== topic=RR-ORD-16 from=SS-T248
T: Leave from the islands: where does it start and end?
A: Your leave begins and ends at Embarkation HQ Kolkata or Chennai. Travel time counts as duty.
K: Applies to officers serving in A&N with homes on the mainland
K: Moved by the quickest means: ship or service aircraft
C: MoD letter A/14209/AG/PS2(B)/188-S-D(AG-ii) dt 16-02-1972, as amended

=== topic=RR-ORD-16 from=SS-T250
T: Islands' residents serving on the mainland: free sea passage
A: Free sea passage by requisition between Kolkata or Chennai and the islands, and back.
K: Voyage time and enforced halts count as duty
C: MoD letter C/01632/Mov C/4295/D(Mov) dt 04-08-1977
