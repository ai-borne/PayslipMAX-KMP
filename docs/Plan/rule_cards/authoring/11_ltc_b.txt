# LTC batch B: Anywhere-in-India LTC (177B) and family rules (SS-T100..SS-T126). Written from TR 2014 Rule 177 first.

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T100
T: Can I start my LTC journey from another city?
A: No. Start from your duty station and go directly, by the shortest route, to your declared destination.
K: The claim is limited to the shortest direct route from duty station
K: A detour through another city is not paid
C: Rule 177B(i)(d), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T101,SS-T106,SS-T133
T: LTC by air: can I break the journey or stay en route?
A: No. Book one ticket for each leg. A break in journey is not admissible, except a connecting flight.
K: One ticket per leg, even with a change of flight
K: An en-route stay is not allowed on air LTC
K: Two separate tickets: only the through-ticket fare is admitted
W: Rail LTC differs: linked warrants are allowed for breaks (177B)
C: DoPT OM 31011/12/2022-Estt.A-IV dt 29-08-2022; Rule 177B(iii) TR
O: The DoPT OM text confirms one ticket per leg only. The 'no en-route stay' position comes from the FAQ; find its source.

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T124,SS-T126
T: Family lives elsewhere: LTC from where they live?
A: Yes. LTC for your wife and children runs from where they live to your leave station and back.
K: When you serve at a non-family station or have no family accommodation
K: Claim is limited to entitled mode and class by the shortest route
H: Your reasons for the family living elsewhere
W: The Controlling Authority checks those reasons
C: Rule 177B(i)(c) and (d), TR 2014; CGDA letter 4007/AT-P dt 05-05-1976

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T103
T: LTC air ticket bought from an unauthorised agent
A: The air fare is not admitted. The claim passes for zero and that year's LTC counts as used.
K: Tickets must come from the three authorised agents
K: Only unavoidable cases can be relaxed, by a senior authority
C: DoE OM 19024/03/2021-E.IV dt 31-12-2021

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T104,SS-T123
T: Family's LTC journey: warrant or fare refund?
A: You travel on warrant. Your wife and children are refunded the actual fare they paid.
K: No railway warrant for the family under 177B
K: Refund has no distance limit, each direction
K: Fare is by your entitled mode and class
C: Rule 177B(i)(g), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T107
T: Anywhere-in-India LTC: can parents, brothers or sisters travel?
A: No. 177B covers only the officer, spouse and children.
K: Not covered: dependent parents, step-parents, brothers, sisters
K: Parents travel under Home Town LTC (177A) instead
C: Rule 177B(i)(a), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T109,SS-T118
T: Newly married wife joining me: what travel do I get?
A: One-way LTC under 177B for her journey from your home town to the station where you serve.
K: Return to your HQ if she gives up that year's own LTC
K: Applies in peace, field and modified areas with married accommodation
W: No baggage, scooter or car conveyance for her move
W: Marrying after your posting gives no permanent-move TA for her
C: Rule 177B(i)(a) Note and (f), and Rule 16, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T120
T: I married while serving in a field area: wife's LTC?
A: Her LTC runs from her residence, treated like an SPR, to your leave station.
K: Limited to fare from the rail head or airport nearest you
K: If you take no LTC, her residence is still the base
C: Rule 177B(i)(e), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T111,SS-T122
T: Wife and children's return journey: the six-month rule
A: The return must finish within six months of the onward journey, or only the onward fare is paid.
K: Return in the next calendar year needs sanction from the Competent Authority
K: Heads of Branches (Service HQ) or GOC-in-C (commands) may relax the limit
C: Rule 177B(i)(a), TR 2014; CGDA letter 4007/AT-P dt 05-05-1976

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T112
T: Encashing leave with LTC: how much?
A: You can encash 10 days of annual leave with each LTC, up to 60 days over your career.
K: Encashed leave does not reduce what you can encash at retirement
K: Both spouses serving: each gets up to 60 days
K: No limit on the number of spells
W: Encashment can be allowed up to 60 days before the outward journey
C: Rule 177B Notes 1 to 3, TR 2014
D: The 60-day career cap applies to each spouse from 01-09-2008.

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T113
T: Spouse's LTC destination must match mine
A: Your spouse travels to the same leave station as you. Going beyond it is not covered.
K: Passing through your leave station to a farther place is not admissible
K: Your spouse still has an independent title
C: Rule 177B(i)(a), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T114
T: Can my family travel in batches?
A: Yes, in any number of batches. One member cannot get the concession twice in a block.
K: No limit on the number of batches
C: Rule 177B(i)(a), TR 2014; CGDA letter 4007/AT-P dt 05-05-1976

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T115,SS-T125
T: Year-end leave: which year does my family's LTC count against?
A: If you and your family travel together next year, their LTC counts against the year yours counts.
K: Applies when your leave is granted late in the year
K: Next calendar year's own LTC is not affected
C: Rule 177B(i)(b) and Rule 177A(v), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T117
T: LTC by bus, steamer or car: what is refunded?
A: For places connected by rail, only up to the rail warrant cost in your entitled class.
K: Private chartered buses and vans: not reimbursed
K: State or public-sector charter buses: actual cost, up to warrant fare
K: Private cars: not reimbursed, whoever owns them
C: Rule 177B(ii) and Notes 1 and 2, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-LTC-02 from=SS-T119
T: LTC when I will not return from leave
A: Not admissible if it is known at departure that you will not return to duty after the leave.
K: Applies to Anywhere-in-India LTC
C: Rule 177B(vi), TR 2014
