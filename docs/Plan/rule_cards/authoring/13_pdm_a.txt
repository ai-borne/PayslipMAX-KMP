# Permanent move batch A: classification, CTG-adjacent, baggage, cars, transfers on leave. Written from TR 2014 Rules 56, 67, 81, 82, 85, 85A, 86-90 and the 2017-18 MoD letters.

=== topic=RR-PDM-01 from=SS-T253,SS-T268
T: What counts as a permanent duty move?
A: A move expected to last over 180 days. A temporary move becomes permanent once it passes 180 days.
K: The ordering authority classifies it and records it in the order
K: Once the move has started, the classification is not revised
K: Courses: counted per station, not by total course length
C: Rule 56 and Rule 91, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T255
T: Peace to field posting: family's air travel?
A: Your family's air fare is paid to your SPR, yours to the new duty station, if other conditions are met.
K: Your fare: old duty station to new duty station, shortest route
K: Family's fare: old duty station to your SPR
K: Family's air entitlement mirrors yours
C: Rule 67(a)(ii) and Rule 62, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T256
T: No government accommodation at the new station: extra fare?
A: Yes. You get an additional fare to the old station and back, with proof of non-availability.
K: Applies when family or household stays behind for want of accommodation
H: Non-availability certificate from the time you joined
H: Allotment letter when accommodation is later given
C: Rule 67(a)(i)(5), TR 2014; MoD letter 12630/TA/DA/Mov C/3003/D(Mov)/2006 dt 16-10-2006

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T257
T: Two own cars: can both be moved at government cost?
A: No. Only one own car can be transported at government cost, by truck or self-propelled.
K: Officers' scale is one motor car, or one motorcycle or scooter
K: Actual cost is paid for carriage by rail, steamer or craft
C: Rule 67(e), TR 2014
O: The FAQ cites 67(v); the TR text shows the car scale under clause (e). Confirm the numbering before publishing.

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T258,SS-T263
T: Leave-cum-posting journey: one claim or two?
A: Two claims: LTC for the leg to your home town, permanent-move TA for the leg to the new station.
K: Old station to home town: LTC under Rule 177A
K: Home town to new station: permanent move under Rule 67
D: Posting via a leave station: you get TA by the direct route old to new, plus LTC for the two legs through the leave station. Family gets LTC only.
C: Rule 177A and Rule 67, TR 2014
O: The handbook paragraph continues with the case where one LTC leg is unused (TA under Rule 85); read it in full before extending.

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T259,SS-T262
T: Both spouses serving: baggage and car on transfer?
A: Moved from two places to one: 6,000 kg each. Moved from one place apart: 6,000 kg between you.
K: Each can move their own car if both need one
K: Posted together, moved to different stations: both get full CTG and TA
K: Children's TA goes to the parent they travel with
C: Rule 82(a) and (b), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-ORD-06 from=SS-T260
T: Both spouses serving: how is CTG split?
A: Transfers within 60 days of each other: one CTG only. After that, the later spouse gets 50%.
K: Within 60 days of each other: only one CTG is paid
K: After 60 days but within six months: 50% to the later spouse
K: After six months: both get the full CTG
W: No CTG for a transfer at your own request
C: Rule 82(a) and Rule 67(c)(iii), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T261 chips=AMENDED
T: Move within the same city: what do I get?
A: No TA or CTG unless your residence changes. If it does: actual costs and one-third CTG.
K: Same station: municipality or corporation limits, with contiguous suburbs and cantonments
K: Conveyance and baggage: actual cost, capped as under Rule 67
K: CTG: one-third, once you actually change residence
C: Rule 81, TR 2014; MoD letter 12630/Mov C/242/D(Mov)/2017 dt 15-09-2017
O: TA handbook says one-third of pay in the pay level; the 2017 letter and FAQ say one-third of the CTG (80% of basic). The letter was followed; confirm.

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T264,SS-T267
T: Posted while on leave: can I return to my old station?
A: No, unless it is ordered in the public interest. Recall only to hand over charge carries no TA.
K: Recalled only to hand over charge or collect kit: no TA
K: The competent leave authority may allow you to rejoin, in public interest
K: CTG stays admissible in these cases
C: Rule 88, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-GEN-03 from=SS-T265
T: Do I send advance copies of movement orders to PCDA(O)?
A: No. Attach a copy of the movement order to each TA/DA claim instead.
K: Applies to temporary and permanent duty moves
C: AO 681/1973 and 130/1975

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T266
T: Leave after a course of under three months: TA?
A: Fare from the course station to the leave station is capped at one-way LTC.
K: To the leave station: actual expense, up to one-way LTC if admissible
K: Back to your permanent station: LTC one way, plus the course-to-station fare
C: Rule 90, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T270
T: Posted while on temporary duty away from HQ: TA?
A: TD-scale TA to the new station, plus the difference between permanent and TD scales.
K: From the temporary HQ to the new duty station: TD scale
K: Direct journey from old to new station: the scale difference
W: If you rejoin the old station first, attach that order
C: Rule 86(a), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T271,SS-T269
T: Posted while on long leave: baggage and car moved?
A: The Controlling Officer may authorise a warrant for baggage and car left at your old station.
K: Within the authorised scale
K: Extra cost of a longer journey: TD-scale TA may be granted
K: Also covers some transfers during sick leave
C: Rule 89, TR 2014; CGDA letter 4030/AT-P dt 20-02-1970

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T272
T: Posted while on short leave (up to four months)
A: You get the conveyance you would have got had you stayed on duty.
K: Leave up to four months, order received before or after leave starts
K: Lower class from your leave station: the fare you paid
C: Rule 87, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-05 from=SS-T191,SS-T279
T: Field posting, married accommodation not allotted: baggage?
A: Baggage and car are reimbursed only with proof of government married accommodation allotted at the field station.
K: Applies to officers drawing Field or Modified Field Allowance
K: No proof needed if you draw CI Ops Peace allowance
K: Posted out later: claim with the accommodation vacation report
C: Rule 85A, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-05 from=SS-T196
T: Non-family field station: family travel and baggage?
A: Not admissible at government cost, even if you arrange your own accommodation.
K: Applies to field areas where families are not permitted
C: AI 146/66; CGDA UO AT/IV/4390-II dt 31-03-1987

=== topic=RR-ORD-06 from=SS-T192 chips=AMENDED
T: Single officer, peace to field: baggage allowed?
A: Up to 5,900 kg to your home town, plus 100 kg on warrant: 6,000 kg in all.
K: 100 kg goes with you on warrant
K: The rest goes to your home town or SPR
K: Total must stay within your maximum entitlement
C: MoD letter 12630/TA/DA/Mov C/13/D(Mov)/18 dt 18-04-2018; Rule 67(d)(vii) TR

=== topic=RR-ORD-06 from=SS-T194,SS-T278 chips=RATES,AMENDED
T: Baggage by rail or road: how much and what rate?
A: Level 10 and above: up to 6,000 kg by goods train or container, or road at a per-km rate.
K: Level 12 and above: double container; Levels 10 to 11: single container
K: Road base rate Rs 50 per km, plus the DA-linked 25% rise
W: Receipts and vouchers are needed for the claim
C: MoD letter 12630/Mov C/242/D(Mov)/2017 dt 15-09-2017; Rule 61A, TR 2014
D: Rates follow the HRA city class (X, Y, Z). Higher X and Y rates apply to moves between X or Y and Z cities. Rule 61A Note 3 also raises rates 25% whenever DA rises 50%.
O: The FAQ shows Rs 62.5/km (base x 1.25). Confirm the escalation order and date before stating a rupee figure.
