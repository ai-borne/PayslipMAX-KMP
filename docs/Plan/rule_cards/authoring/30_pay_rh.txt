# Pay batch 1: Risk and Hardship allowance (SS-P057..SS-P077). Written from the P&A handbook ch. 15 and the 2022 MoD (DMA) letter as cited.

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P060,SS-P062,SS-P063 chips=RATES
T: Risk and Hardship allowance: how it works
A: A location-based allowance from 22-02-2019. Your rate comes from your station's cell in the R&H matrix.
K: Station cells run from R1H1 (highest) to R3H3 (lowest)
K: Not paid with field, CI or Tough Location allowances
K: Your unit's eligibility comes from the Corps Notification
C: MoD (DMA) letter 8(3)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 21-04-2022; ADG PS letter C/7021/PAY/SAPCS/2022 dt 24-04-2022
D: Monthly rates in rupees: R1H1 25,000; R1H2 16,900; R2H1 16,900; R2H2 10,500; R1H3 5,300; R3H1 5,300; R2H3 3,400; R3H2 3,400; R3H3 1,200.

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P062,SS-P063 id=RB-SS-P062-b
T: Claiming R&H: the Part II orders
A: Your unit publishes a CEASE order for the old rate and a GRANT order for the revised rate, from 22-02-2019.
K: The GRANT order quotes the unit's serial number in the Corps Notification
K: Retired or deceased cases: the unit publishes CEASE and GRANT orders
K: Send them to the Record Office or ORO
H: Certificate that the 1994 and 2022 letters' conditions are met
H: Your station code, and the unit's entry in the Corps Notification
C: MoD letter 37269/AG/PS3(a)/90/D(Pay/Services) dt 13-01-1994; MoD (DMA) letter dt 21-04-2022

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P067,SS-P072,SS-P074,SS-P077 chips=RATES
T: R&H one cell down: which units?
A: Formation HQs up to Division, static units, MES, farms, recruiting, training centres, NCC and record offices.
K: One cell lower: R1H1 becomes R2H1; R1H2 becomes R2H2
K: 77% of R1H2 drops to R2H2; 60% of R2H2 drops to R3H2
K: Only for locations listed in the 21-04-2022 letter
W: If it would cut your existing allowance, the existing one continues
C: MoD (DMA) letter 8(3)/2017/D(Pay/Services) dt 21-04-2022

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P068,SS-P069
T: My location fell to a lower R&H cell: what do I get?
A: A location that earned more before 21-04-2022 keeps it. You may claim the more beneficial allowance.
K: A location missing from the letter: you keep the existing allowance
K: Modified Field Area allowance continues at the present rate
C: MoD (DMA) letter dt 21-04-2022

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P058,SS-P059,SS-P064,SS-P070,SS-P071
T: R&H at my location: does my Peace or Field status change?
A: No. R&H changes only the allowance. Your unit stays peace or field for every other purpose.
K: Peace units get no other Field Service Concessions
K: Field and CI units stay so for other purposes
K: HAA Cat III and HAFA at R1H1 rates are paid together
C: MoD (DMA) letter dt 21-04-2022

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P057
T: Siachen: did R&H change my allowance?
A: No. Siachen does not appear in the 21-04-2022 letter, so your entitlement is unchanged.
K: Siachen Allowance continues as before
C: MoD (DMA) letter dt 21-04-2022

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P076
T: Strategic Forces units: CFAA or R&H?
A: CFAA, not R&H, irrespective of location.
K: Publish Part II orders for CFAA and not for R&H
C: Para 2.4, MoD (DMA) letter dt 21-04-2022

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P061,SS-P065,SS-P066,SS-P073
T: R&H during absence: leave, TD, course, hospital
A: Paid through 20 days of casual leave and 3 months of TD. Not on annual leave or a course of 10 weeks or more.
K: Other leave, even a day, stops it; CL, sick leave excepted
K: A course does not count as TD for this purpose
K: A course over 10 weeks: no R&H
C: MoD letter 37269/AG/PS3(a)/90/D(Pay/Services) dt 13-01-1994; MoD letter PC-1(16)/2017/D(Pay/Services)/Pt.III dt 24-01-2020

=== topic=RP-055 from=SS-P075
T: What is concurrent admissibility of allowances?
A: Two or more allowances paid at the same time, as the MoD letter of 29-02-2000 explains.
K: R&H itself is not paid with field, CI or Tough Location allowances
C: MoD letter 1(26)97/D(Pay/Services) dt 29-02-2000
