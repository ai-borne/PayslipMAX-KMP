# Permanent move batch B: modes, first appointment, families, MNS (SS-T273..SS-T288, SS-T184). Written from TR 2014 Rules 61A, 64, 65, 67, 79, 83, 85, 85A, 178, 182.

=== topic=RR-PDM-02 from=SS-T273,SS-T274,SS-T151
T: How do I travel on a permanent posting?
A: Your travel entitlement is the same as for temporary duty, mode by mode.
K: Rail: on warrant, unless the competent authority allows cash payment
K: Cash payment: fare of the class travelled or entitled, whichever is less
K: Own accord without warrant: cost of warrant or actual, whichever is less
C: Rule 67(a)(i) and Rule 47(iii), TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-03 from=SS-T275,SS-T277
T: Newly commissioned: travel to my first station
A: AC II on warrant from home or your academy, family by direct route, and 225 kg baggage on warrant.
K: Family: free conveyance from your home to the permanent station
K: Baggage up to 225 kg, including 55 kg from the training unit
K: Daily allowance for the journey period; no CTG
C: Rule 64 and Rule 65, TR 2014; CGDA letter 4199/AT-P dt 09-04-1973

=== topic=RR-PDM-03 from=SS-T276
T: Re-employed after retirement: travel to my first station
A: AC II on warrant for you and your family, from your home to the reporting station.
K: Re-employed within six months: unclaimed retirement TA can be claimed later
K: Lower class if there is no AC II on the line
C: Rule 64, Rule 201 and Rule 206, TR 2014; SAI 1/S/80

=== topic=RR-PDM-04 from=SS-T278
T: Baggage carried on free government transport
A: What the free transport carries is deducted from your entitlement. Any excess is recovered.
K: Applies when free road transport is provided on transfer
K: Recovery is at the rate you would have been reimbursed
C: Rule 79, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-05 from=SS-T280
T: Married while in a field area: wife's move to a peace station
A: Her conveyance is paid under Rule 85, limited to the lower of two costs.
K: Rail head nearest your old field station to the new peace station
K: Or her residence to the new peace station, if less
K: Baggage conveyance is allowed from her place of residence
C: Rule 85, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-05 from=SS-T281
T: My family moves before or after me: what is paid?
A: Warrant, or the lower of the entitled-class fare and the actual fare. Officers' families may fly.
K: Baggage up to your maximum scale, less what you carry yourself
K: Government liability stops at the old-to-new station distance
C: Rule 85 and Rule 16, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-05 from=SS-T282
T: Family joining me in government married accommodation in a field area
A: One-time free warrant for your family and baggage from their residence, once accommodation is allotted.
K: Only government-built married accommodation, not hired or requisitioned
K: Baggage limited to what was first moved to the SPR
K: Valid only while the station is a family station
C: Rule 85A, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-06 from=SS-T283
T: Officer lent to another department: whose travel rules apply?
A: Those of the lending or borrowing department, depending on whose pay scale you draw.
K: The borrowing department bears the cost
K: Unless the loan letter says otherwise
C: Rule 83, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-PDM-07 from=SS-T284
T: MNS officer: my husband's travel entitlements
A: He is part of your family and gets its facilities, unless his own employer gives the same.
K: No proof of dependency and no income limit
K: You must give details of his job
C: Rule 2, TR 2014

=== topic=RR-ORD-02 from=SS-T285
T: Can a Lieutenant travel by air on TD?
A: Yes. All commissioned officers, including MNS officers, are entitled to travel by air.
K: Levels 5A to 11 and Levels 12 to 13B: economy class
K: Level 14 and above: business or club class
C: MoD letter 12630/Mov C/242/D(Mov)/2017 dt 15-09-2017; Rule 62 TR

=== topic=RR-PDM-07 from=SS-T286,SS-T287,SS-T288,SS-T209
T: MNS officers: TA/DA, LTC and concession vouchers
A: Same TA/DA as other service officers. LTC is the same, but with Form G at 50% fare.
K: Form G: 50% of the fare, including reservation charges
K: Six one-way journeys a year, like Form D
K: Form D is not available to MNS officers
C: Rule 178 and Rule 182, TR 2014; MoD letter B/83878/DGAFMS/MNS/2204/D(Mov) dt 01-06-1981
D: Family members of MNS officers may use Form 'D', but Form 'D' and Form G together are capped at six a calendar year.

=== topic=RR-TD-02 from=SS-T184 chips=GUIDANCE
T: Missed connection, night in a transit city: reimbursed?
A: Yes. Hotel rent, conveyance and food are payable for an enforced halt.
K: Applies on temporary duty when no connecting flight is available that day
